InsightIQ AI is an intelligent data analytics platform that allows users to upload CSV datasets, visualize data interactively, and ask AI-powered analytical questions in natural language.

The platform combines:

📊 Interactive analytics dashboards
🤖 AI-powered business insights
📈 Dynamic charts and KPI monitoring
🧠 RAG-based intelligent querying
🗂 Multi-dataset support

to provide a modern Business Intelligence experience similar to Power BI and AI analytics copilots.
